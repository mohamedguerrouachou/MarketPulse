The problem

Competitor prices move all day. Spot checks can't keep up.

A price pulled once in the morning tells you nothing about the evening surge. To understand how a competitor actually prices a market, you need real quotes, on the same routes, at the same times, every single day — at a volume no one can check by hand.

"What did they charge for this exact trip, right now?" The question pricing teams keep needing answered — reliably, not occasionally.
The solution

MarketPulse asks the question for you — continuously.

It holds a standing schedule of real trips across each market and puts the exact question — pickup, destination, time of day — directly to the competitor's own quoting engine. Every answer is logged automatically, all day, without anyone touching it.

Hundreds, up to thousands of quotes a day, on autopilot. No manual lookups, no spreadsheets, no gaps in coverage.
The bigger picture

MarketPulse consists of modules within a larger project.

The same schedule → ask → verify → deliver approach is built once per competitor. Each one runs as its own independent module feeding the same shared intelligence, so coverage grows one competitor at a time without changing how the others work.

How it works

Four steps, repeating all day, every day.

01

Schedule

A standing list of trips per market, each with its own time of day to check.

02

Ask

At the right moment, MarketPulse requests a live quote for that exact trip.

03

Verify

No answer in time? It automatically tries again before moving on.

04

Deliver

Confirmed quotes are bundled and dropped into one shared dataset.
